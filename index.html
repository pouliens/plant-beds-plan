L‑Shape Courtyard Raised Bed

Reference plan for a square UK courtyard with two beds running along two walls. Designed to avoid damp risk by keeping soil away from the building wall.

Best layout: L‑shape Keep 50–100mm dry gap Keep soil 150mm below DPC Drainage required Brick/block preferred

Courtyard layout

Recommended build-up

Use a freestanding brick/block bed, not soil pushed directly against the building.

Building wallLeave untouched. Do not bridge damp proof course or cover air bricks.
Dry gap50–100mm gravel inspection gap between wall and bed.
Inner wallFreestanding brick/block wall in front of the building wall.
Soil zoneTopsoil + peat-free compost + grit. Line sides, not bottom.
Drainage baseCoarse gravel/rubble over existing paving/concrete, with weep holes.
Warning: Keep finished soil level at least 150mm below the damp proof course. Do not block vents, drains, air bricks or inspection covers.

Build sequence

  1. 1

    Survey walls.
    Find DPC, vents, air bricks, drain covers and any slope toward the house.

  2. 2

    Mark the L‑shape.
    Leave a 50–100mm dry gap along both walls and keep enough walking space.

  3. 3

    Prepare base.
    Clean paving/concrete. Create a level mortar bed or shallow foundation strip if needed.

  4. 4

    Build walls.
    Build front wall and inner wall as freestanding brick/block structure.

  5. 5

    Add drainage.
    Use weep holes/open vertical joints low down, backed with gravel and geotextile.

  6. 6

    Line sides.
    Line soil-facing sides only. Do not fully seal the bottom.

  7. 7

    Fill and plant.
    Add gravel base, soil mix, then plants. Keep the wall gap clear.

Best approach (UK + B&Q friendly)

  1. 1

    Measure first, then buy once.
    Measure both wall runs, decide bed height (300–450mm), and calculate brick/soil volumes before ordering.

  2. 2

    Split into two trips or one delivery.
    At B&Q, pre-order heavy items (bricks, gravel, topsoil) for delivery; collect small items/tools in store.

  3. 3

    Build structure before planting spend.
    Complete wall, drainage and dry gap first; only then buy plants so they are not sitting in pots too long.

Tip: keep ~10% contingency for breakages/cuts and an extra bag of mortar and gravel.
